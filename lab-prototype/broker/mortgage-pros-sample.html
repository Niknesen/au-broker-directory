About Mortgage Pros

Founded in Sydney’s Inner West, Mortgage Pros is an accredited finance brokerage led by senior lending specialists Eric and Patrick. With extensive experience across residential property finance, commercial borrowing, and government grant consulting, the team delivers clear, disciplined, and transparent credit guidance.

Operating an accredited panel of 38 bank and non-bank lenders, Mortgage Pros sets itself apart by conducting thorough upfront loan structuring. They specialize in unlocking borrowing capacity for complex client profiles—including 1-year ABN sole traders, low-deposit First Home Guarantee buyers, and investors structuring multi-property SMSF portfolios.

Across more than 1,500 independently verified client settlements, the firm maintains an audited 99.4% settlement approval rate. Clients benefit from guaranteed 1.5-hour priority response SLAs and direct communication before paperwork is submitted to credit assessors.
