Frequently Asked Questions

How are business brokers evaluated on Best Brokers Australia?

Every broker profile is independently evaluated using our 3-factor Trust Score methodology: aggregate Google review rating (50%), verified review volume (30%), and public regulatory / licence disclosure on their official site (20%). We do not accept payment to alter rankings.

How much does it cost to use an Australian business broker?

Most mortgage and finance brokers in Australia do not charge upfront borrower fees, as they are remunerated by the lender upon loan settlement. For insurance and commercial services, fee structures vary and must be disclosed upfront in a Financial Services Guide (FSG).

How do I verify if a business broker is licensed in Australia?

All credit representatives and Australian Credit License (ACL) or Australian Financial Services License (AFSL) holders are registered on ASIC's professional registers. Look for their licence number on their profile card or official website.
