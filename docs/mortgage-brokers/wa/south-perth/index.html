Frequently Asked Questions: South Perth Lending

How much do mortgage & finance brokers charge in South Perth WA?
In Australia, most mortgage broker services are free for standard retail and residential clients, as commissions are paid directly by the financial institution upon settlement. Under Australian Credit and Financial Services laws, brokers are legally bound to act in your best interests (Best Interests Duty).

Are brokers in South Perth WA licensed with ASIC?
Yes. All accredited Australian finance and mortgage brokers operating in South Perth must hold an Australian Credit Licence (ACL) or be an authorized Credit Representative under an aggregator, registered with the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC).
