Frequently Asked Questions: Geelong Lending

How much do mortgage & finance brokers charge in Geelong VIC?
In Australia, most mortgage broker services are free for standard retail and residential clients, as commissions are paid directly by the financial institution upon settlement. Under Australian Credit and Financial Services laws, brokers are legally bound to act in your best interests (Best Interests Duty).

Are brokers in Geelong VIC licensed with ASIC?
Yes. All accredited Australian finance and mortgage brokers operating in Geelong must hold an Australian Credit Licence (ACL) or be an authorized Credit Representative under an aggregator, registered with the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC).
