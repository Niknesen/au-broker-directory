Frequently Asked Questions: Parramatta Mortgage Lending

How much does a mortgage broker cost in Parramatta, Australia?
In Australia, mortgage brokers are almost always 100% free for residential borrowers. Brokers are remunerated via upfront and trail commissions paid directly by the chosen lender (CBA, Westpac, Macquarie, etc.) upon loan settlement. Under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP), Australian brokers are legally bound by the Best Interests Duty (BID) to recommend loans that benefit you, not the lender.
Mortgage Broker vs Direct to Bank: Which gets lower interest rates in Sydney?
Brokers typically secure sharper rates than branch lenders because they compare 30+ bank and non-bank panels simultaneously. Additionally, accredited brokers have access to unpublished discretionary rate discounts (often 0.20% to 0.45% below advertised branch rates) and can leverage multiple lenders to waive Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI).
Can a Parramatta broker help me use the NSW First Home Buyer Assistance Scheme?
Yes. Parramatta brokers regularly process applications under the NSW First Home Buyer Assistance Scheme (which provides complete stamp duty exemptions on properties up to $800,000 and concessions up to $1,000,000) as well as the Federal Government Home Guarantee Scheme (5% deposit with zero LMI).
How do self-employed or low-doc home loans work in Western Sydney?
If you are self-employed or run an ABN in Western Sydney with less than 2 years of full financials, specialist Parramatta brokers submit 'Alt-Doc' loans using 12 months of BAS statements, business bank statements, or an accountant's declaration through second-tier non-bank lenders such as Pepper Money, Liberty, or Resimac.
