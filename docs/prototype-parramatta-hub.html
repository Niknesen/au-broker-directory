Frequently Asked Questions: Parramatta Mortgage Brokers

Do mortgage brokers in Parramatta charge fees to borrowers?
In almost all standard residential cases, no. Australian mortgage brokers are paid a commission directly by the chosen lender upon loan settlement. Under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act (NCCP) Best Interests Duty (BID), they are legally obligated to act in your best financial interest.
Which Parramatta broker has the highest Google rating?
Based on our aggregated database of 9,616 reviews, Palise Property ranks #1 with a 5 ★ rating across 683 verified client reviews and a Trust Score of 95/100.
Can Parramatta brokers help with the NSW First Home Buyer Guarantee?
Yes. Most accredited brokers in Parramatta have access to Federal Home Guarantee Scheme allocations (5% deposit with no LMI) and can structure stamp duty exemptions under NSW First Home Buyer Assistance scheme.
